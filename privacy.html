Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Pilot release

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, password (stored hashed), phone number, language preference.
  • Profile: business name, trades, service area, license number, insurance expiry and any certificate you upload, years in business, plan.
  • Activity: jobs you post, bids, questions, messages, reviews, properties and access notes you add, photos you upload, deals you redeem, and a log of which contact details you looked at.
  • Technical: the usual server logs (IP address, browser, time) kept by our hosting providers.

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show listings, deliver bids and messages, notify you about your jobs.
  • To send you texts and emails about your own activity (a new bid, a hire, a question). You can turn texts off under More. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop.
  • To keep the Service safe: rate-limit contact lookups, detect scraping and spam, review insurance documents, and act on reports.
  • To bill paid plans, once billing begins.

Who sees what

  • Anyone, signed in or not, can see contractor and store listings (name, trades, area, badges, ratings) and open job summaries (trade, city, when).
  • Signed-in members can see phone numbers on a per-lookup basis, with a daily limit.
  • Your bids are seen only by the person who posted the job. Your property address and access notes are seen only by a contractor you have hired and chosen to share them with.
  • Seller leads ("Sell my home") are seen by CallSheet and may be passed to local investors or agents we work with, which is the purpose of the form.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We store data with Supabase (database and file storage, United States) and host the site on GitHub Pages. When enabled, Twilio delivers text messages, Stripe processes payments, and Cloudflare provides bot protection. Each handles data under its own privacy terms.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile and contact details any time.
  • Turn off text messages under More.
  • Delete your account under More → Delete my account. This removes your profile and any organization you were the only member of. Reviews you left may be kept in anonymized form; records we must keep for legal or billing reasons are kept for that period.
  • Email us to ask what we hold about you or to correct it.

Children

CallSheet is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We will post changes here and note the effective date.
